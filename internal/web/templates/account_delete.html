What is removed

  • Your account row: username, email address, password hash, locale and appearance preferences, and your OIDC link.
  • Every way into the account: browser sessions, API tokens, password-reset tokens, push subscriptions and per-user notification channels.
  • Notification history rows attributed to your account, and the username in audit entries that named you (relabelled deleted-user).

What stays

Datey stores one shared address book, so the people, birthdays, anniversaries, groups, notes and gift ideas are not owned by, or deleted with, a single account. They remain available to the other people who use this instance. If you added a person record that should be removed, delete that person separately from their detail page (administrators can do this too).

Backups taken before the deletion may still contain the old account data until they rotate out; they are not used to restore individual accounts.

Delete a person who has no account

Datey stores people who may never have an account of their own. To remove one of those records, open the person's page and choose Delete person. That deletes their events, notes, gift ideas, photo and relationships; it does not affect any account.

The full detail is in the privacy policy.
